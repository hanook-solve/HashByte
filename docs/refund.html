Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

If HashByte doesn't work for you, request a refund within 14 days of purchase. To request one, email the address on your Lemon Squeezy receipt or open a support request through the store.

Eligibility

  • Requested within 14 days of purchase
  • One refund per license key
  • Duplicate or accidental purchases are refunded in full

Processing

Refunds are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record, and typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days. Once refunded, the license key is deactivated.

Contact

For refund requests or issues, use GitHub Issues or your Lemon Squeezy receipt email.
