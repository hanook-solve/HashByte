HashByte

Drop your images in. Get smaller files out.

HashByte is a small, fast Windows desktop app that compresses JPEG, PNG, and WEBP images — either shrinking them by quality, or hitting an exact target file size in KB.

  • Batch-compress whole folders in one go
  • General mode: reduce size while keeping quality
  • Target mode: hit an exact KB size (e.g. for ID photo uploads)
  • No account, no upload — everything runs locally on your PC
  • Single .exe, auto-updates itself
  • 500 compressions free every month, no license required

How it works

Open HashByte, drag in your images or a folder, pick a format, and press compress. Files are processed on your machine and never leave your computer.
